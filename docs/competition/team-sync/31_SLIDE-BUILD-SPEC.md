# PART 3 — EMPHASIS

Five things should be loud. Everything else supports them.

1. **The three numbered headers** — they carry the structure
2. **`56 %` and `400+`** in the column-2 sub
3. **`no RCPs, no seals, no boron plant, no surge line`** — bold this whole clause; it is the single
   strongest sentence on the slide
4. **`Fuji / Toshiba / MHI`** — bold the names. This is the literal answer to the question that was asked
5. **`48 Turkish firms in ENR Top 250 — 2nd in the world`** — bold `2nd in the world`

**Colour:** deck accent on the three column headers and the `WHERE WE ORDER` label only. Strip boxes get
a light neutral fill, all five identical — do not accent one, they are a set. Keep the footer grey.

**The design job of this slide:** the eye should go *headers → strip*. The three points are the argument;
the strip is the proof that the argument is concrete. If the strip looks like a footnote, the slide fails.

# PART 5 — IF IT IS TOO DENSE

Cut in this order. **Never cut the strip** — it is the answer to the question.

1. Drop the subtitle
2. Drop the third bullet in columns 1 and 3
3. Shorten the turbine box to `Fuji / Toshiba / MHI class — all three supply Turkish geothermal`
4. Drop the footer and say the delivery model out loud instead
5. Last resort: merge strip boxes 4 and 5 into `FUEL, I&C + ELECTRICAL`

**If you need a two-slide version:** three points on slide A, the full sourcing table from
`33_SOURCING-AND-CONSTRUCTION.md` Part 2 on slide B. Better than shrinking the strip to unreadable.

# PART 7 — THE 45-SECOND WALK-THROUGH

> "Three points on how this gets built.
>
> **One — licence in parallel, not in series.** NDK has been an independent regulator since 2018 and its
> 2023 authorizations regulation already covers SMRs by scope, so the framework exists. We would enter
> pre-licensing in year one, alongside procurement rather than after it, and the November 2025 Türkiye–
> Korea memorandum has a licensing chapter we would use for review credit.
>
> **Two — inherit the supply chain rather than create one.** Akkuyu is at fifty-six percent local content
> with more than four hundred qualified firms and TSE as the material approval body since 2020. What we
> need domestically on day one is exactly what those firms already ship. And the hardest items are
> designed out — no reactor coolant pumps, no seals, no boron plant, no surge line.
>
> **Three — enter through the national programme.** Türkiye has a declared five-gigawatt SMR target and a
> TENMAK domestic reactor development call. Sinop and Thrace are large-reactor tracks with foreign
> vendors; **we are the domestic complement to those, not a rival.**
>
> And to answer the equipment question directly — the strip along the bottom. **Our turbine is a
> forty-megawatt saturated-steam machine, which is the geothermal class: Fuji, Toshiba and Mitsubishi
> have each delivered one this size to a Turkish site, and Turkish geothermal equipment is already about
> fifty-five percent localised.** Vessel under licence-build. Civil and balance of plant domestic —
> forty-eight Turkish contractors are in the ENR top two hundred and fifty, second in the world. Fuel and
> safety-class instrumentation imported, though the boron ore itself is Turkish."
