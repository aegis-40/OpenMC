# Stand Banner — Design Instructions

**Format:** 40 × 50 cm, portrait
**Purpose:** stop a passer-by at 2–3 m and make them walk to the stand
**Language:** copy given below in **English and Turkish** — pick one as primary, or set both (see §6)

---

## 1. The rule that decides everything

A 40 × 50 cm banner is a **glance medium**. You have about **two seconds** as someone walks past. Everything below follows from that:

- **One message.** Not three, not a summary of the project.
- **Numbers, not sentences.** People remember "6 years". Nobody remembers a bullet list.
- **Zero technical vocabulary.** No acronyms, no units a non-engineer must decode.
- **The image is the hook.** Eyes go to objects before text.

**Sizing rule used throughout: 1 cm of capital-letter height ≈ 3 m of readable distance.**
So a 3 cm headline reads at ~9 m; 1 cm body text reads at ~3 m and is invisible beyond that.

---

## 2. Production specification

| | |
|---|---|
| Trim size | 400 × 500 mm |
| Bleed | 3–5 mm each side (ask your printer) |
| Safe margin | **25–30 mm** — no text or key detail closer to the edge |
| Resolution | **300 dpi at final size** = 4724 × 5906 px |
| Minimum acceptable | 150 dpi = 2362 × 2953 px — below this, edges soften visibly |
| Colour | Design in RGB, let the printer convert to CMYK. Expect deep blues to darken slightly. |
| Finish | **Matte.** Gloss mirrors hall lighting and destroys legibility. |
| Material | 5 mm foam board on an easel, or rigid PVC. A roll-up stand is oversized for this format. |
| Mounting height | Centre at **140–150 cm** — standing eye level. Lower and the numbers hide behind people. |

**Check your source image before committing.** For a hero image placed 30 cm tall, you need at least **3543 px** of height at 300 dpi. If your best render is smaller, either place it smaller or re-export from CAD at higher resolution — do not upscale.

---

## 3. Layout

Portrait 400 × 500 mm. **Recommended layout — "tall hero, right"**, because a reactor vessel is naturally a portrait object and a half-cut section reads best vertically.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐  0 mm
│  identity strip (small)              │
│                                      │  30
│  WORDMARK                            │
│  ──────                              │  95
│  hook line                           │
│  power / site credential             │  150
├───────────────────┬──────────────────┤
│                   │                  │  170
│   number 1        │                  │
│                   │    HERO IMAGE    │
│   number 2        │    (vertical     │
│                   │     vessel or    │
│   number 3        │     half-cut)    │
│                   │                  │
├───────────────────┴──────────────────┤  455
│  closing line          team · uni    │  → 500
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Zone | mm (from top) | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | 25–60 | Competition, category, team name — small |
| Wordmark | 60–105 | **AEGIS-40**, cap height **30–35 mm** |
| Rule | ~108 | Short accent bar under the wordmark, 90–100 mm long, 3 mm thick |
| Hook | 118–140 | The one striking line, cap height **9–10 mm** |
| Credential | 145–160 | Power and site, cap height 5–6 mm |
| Numbers column | 175–450, left 30–190 mm | Three stats stacked |
| Hero image | 170–450, right 195–370 mm | Vertical vessel / half-cut |
| Footer band | 470–500 | Closing line + team, on a darker strip |

### Alternate layouts

**B — "hero band across".** Wordmark and hook on top, image in a full-width horizontal band in the middle, three numbers in a row beneath. Use this **only if your best image is landscape**.

**C — "full bleed".** The render fills the whole banner, text overlaid on a darkened area. Very striking, but it needs a high-resolution image with clean empty space for the text to sit on. Do not attempt this with an image that has a white background.

## 4. Typography

| Element | Cap height | ≈ point size | Readable at | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | **30–35 mm** | 85–100 pt | 15 m | Bold |
| Big numbers | **28–32 mm** | 80–90 pt | 9 m | Bold |
| Number unit (YIL/YEARS) | 7–8 mm | 20–22 pt | 2.5 m | Bold |
| Hook line | 9–10 mm | 26–28 pt | 3 m | Bold |
| Number caption | 4–5 mm | 13–15 pt | 1.5 m | Regular |
| Identity / footer | 4–5 mm | 14–15 pt | 1.5 m | Bold |

**One typeface, two weights.** A clean grotesque (Inter, Roboto, Source Sans, Montserrat) is safer than anything decorative. Whatever you choose **must include Turkish characters** — ı İ ş Ş ğ Ğ ü Ü ö Ö ç Ç. Test by typing *"ısıl · yakıt değişimi · güvenli"* before committing.

## 5. Colour

| Role | Suggested | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Deep navy `#0F2847` | An exhibition hall is a sea of white boards under white light — a dark field is the cheapest way not to disappear |
| Hero panel | White `#FFFFFF` | If your image has a white background, put it in a white panel so it blends instead of showing a visible box |
| Wordmark | White | |
| Accent 1 | Orange `#E0801E` | Hook line, first number, closing line |
| Accent 2 | Green `#12905A` | The safety number — reads as "safe" without saying it |
| Secondary text | Muted blue `#9FB3CC` | |

**If your hero image is dark** (a navy vessel render), it will vanish on a navy background. Either put it in a white panel, or invert the whole scheme to a light background with navy type.

## 6. Copy — English and Turkish

Set **one language as primary at full size**, and the other **smaller underneath in the muted colour**. Do not set both at equal size — it doubles the reading time you don't have.

**Recommendation for a Turkish venue:** Turkish primary, English secondary.

| Element | English | Türkçe |
|---|---|---|
| Identity line 1 | TEKNOFEST 2026 · Nuclear Energy Technologies | TEKNOFEST 2026 · Nükleer Enerji Teknolojileri |
| Identity line 2 | Design Competition · Team NUClearly | Tasarım Yarışması · Takım NUClearly |
| **Wordmark** | **AEGIS-40** | **AEGIS-40** |
| **Hook line** | **The nuclear reactor that cools itself** | **Kendini soğutan nükleer reaktör** |
| Credential | 125 MW thermal · 40 MW electric · Sinop | 125 MW ısıl · 40 MW elektrik · Sinop |
| Image caption | Core, steam generator and pressuriser — all inside one vessel | Kor, buhar üreteci ve basınçlandırıcı — hepsi tek bir kabın içinde |
| **Number 1** | **6 YEARS** — runs without refuelling | **6 YIL** — yakıt değişimi olmadan çalışır |
| **Number 2** | **10 DAYS** — cools itself with no electricity | **10 GÜN** — elektrik olmadan kendini soğutur |
| **Number 3** | **3 PRODUCTS** — electricity · heat · hydrogen | **3 ÜRÜN** — elektrik · ısı · hidrojen |
| Closing line | Safe even when the power is cut. | Elektrik kesilse bile güvenli. |
| Footer | NUClearly · New Uzbekistan University | NUClearly · New Uzbekistan University |

### Why these three numbers

- **6 years** — long-cycle operation is instantly graspable and genuinely unusual. Nobody expects a power plant that runs six years without stopping.
- **10 days** — this is your ≥240-hour passive grace period restated in human units. **It is the strongest single fact you own**, because it directly answers the Fukushima association every visitor arrives with.
- **3 products** — hydrogen is publicly and politically attractive, and no competitor on the IAEA list sells three.

## 7. Choosing and preparing the hero image

**Use the half-cut (sectioned) view** if you have a good one — a cutaway is far more interesting to a passer-by than a closed vessel, because it shows there is something inside.

**But strip the callout labels.** Pressurizer / ICRDM / OTSG / Riser / Barrel / Distributor are exactly the technical vocabulary this banner must not have. Either use a version without leader lines, or cover them.

If you want two or three labels, use **plain words only**: *fuel · steam · water* (Türkçe: *yakıt · buhar · su*). Never more than three.

**Preparation checklist:**
- [ ] Crop tight — no wide empty margins around the object
- [ ] Remove or cover every technical callout
- [ ] Check resolution at final placed size (≥ 300 dpi, ≥ 150 dpi absolute floor)
- [ ] If the background is white, either keep it in a white panel or cut it out cleanly — a grey halo around a cut-out looks amateurish at print size
- [ ] Look for stray leader lines or artefacts at the crop edges — they are invisible on screen and obvious at 40 cm

---

## 8. Leave off

- Every acronym: MDNBR, LOCA, IRWST, pcm, k-effective, DNBR, EFPD
- Tables and charts of any kind
- Efficiency, burnup, enrichment, shutdown margin
- The site layout diagram
- Anything that takes more than two seconds

The labelled cutaway, the layout drawing and the numbers all belong **on the stand** for people who stop. The banner's only job is to make them stop.

---

## 9. Pre-print checklist

- [ ] Readable from 3 m — print an A4 test and view it from 60 cm (equivalent viewing angle)
- [ ] Turkish characters render correctly: ı İ ş ğ ü ö ç
- [ ] Nothing within 25 mm of any edge
- [ ] Hero image ≥ 150 dpi at placed size
- [ ] No technical acronym anywhere on the banner
- [ ] Matte finish specified
- [ ] Bleed added if the printer asked for it
- [ ] Everyone on the stand can answer *"how does it cool itself?"* in one sentence:
      *"Gravity and natural circulation — there are no pumps to fail. If it loses all power, cooling continues on its own for more than ten days with nobody touching it."*
