# Teknofest 2026 — Detailed Design Working Notes
## Category
Nuclear Energy Technologies — Detailed Design

## Design candidate
**Aegis-40** (40 MWe integral PWR)

## Program goals
- Demonstrate a modular, economically competitive iPWR concept
- Enable flexible operation via multi-application energy integration
- Maintain strong passive safety and 3S alignment
- Align technical roadmap with Turkey's energy and industrial strategy

## Initial technical threads
1. Core physics and reactivity management without soluble boron
2. Integrated thermal-hydraulic envelope and passive safety behavior
3. TES + SOE coupling for flexible demand and hydrogen production
4. Digital Twin architecture for predictive operation and diagnostics
5. wFOM-based option ranking across performance, safety, economics, and deployability
